FAQ

When does the Gen X Black Friday 2026 sale end?

From 14 November to 30 November 2026. A new deal opens each day and closes when that day ends or its stock runs out, whichever comes first. Offers unlock at midnight; the pre-sale countdown was set to 14 November 2026, 00:00 IST. The page does not print a separate closing clock time for 30 November, so check the live countdown on the last day.

Does the deal price apply to renewals?

Deal prices apply to the first billing term unless the offer says otherwise. The renewal price is shown at checkout before you pay. For domains, the first year is the sale price; renewals are at the regular price shown on the card.

Is GST included in these prices?

The domain deals are listed as excluding GST. The sale page does not say whether hosting, VPS, email or wallet amounts include GST. Confirm the tax line at checkout, and add a GSTIN if you need it on invoices.

Can I get a refund if I buy during the sale?

The sale page lists “30-day money back” on hosting plans. The Terms of Use are narrower: refunds only in the 30-day money-back period for an unresolved issue caused by hosting infrastructure, no refunds on monthly and quarterly contracts, and a 15-day money-back period on half-yearly contracts. Bonus credit cannot be withdrawn or refunded (and is removed if the add-funds payment is reversed). Read the Terms and the checkout; do not assume a blanket refund.

Can I use wallet bonus credit on domains?

No. The sale page says bonus credit is for hosting, VPS, email and apps. It is not valid on domain registrations, transfers or renewals.

Do I need to be an existing customer to add funds or spin?

No. Anyone with a Gen X account can add funds. If you are new, register first, then top up from the client area. Every Gen X account also gets one free spin a day during the sale, with no purchase needed.

Does bonus credit expire?

Bonus credit is valid for 90 days from the top-up that earned it. The money you added yourself never expires.
