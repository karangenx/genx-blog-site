FAQ

When does the Gen X Black Friday 2026 sale end?

From 14 November to 30 November 2026. A new deal opens each day and closes when that day ends or its stock runs out, whichever comes first. Offers unlock at midnight IST. Check the live countdown on the last day.

Does the deal price apply to renewals?

Deal prices apply to the first billing term unless the offer says otherwise. The renewal price is shown at checkout before you pay. For domains, the first year is the sale price; renewals are at the regular price shown on the card.

Is GST included in these prices?

The domain deals are listed as excluding GST. Confirm the tax line at checkout for hosting, VPS, email or wallet top-ups, and add a GSTIN if you need it on invoices.

Can I get a refund if I buy during the sale?

The sale page lists “30-day money back” on hosting plans. The Terms of Use are narrower: refunds only in the 30-day money-back period for an unresolved issue caused by hosting infrastructure, no refunds on monthly and quarterly contracts, and a 15-day money-back period on half-yearly contracts. Bonus credit cannot be withdrawn or refunded (and is removed if the add-funds payment is reversed). Read the Terms and the checkout before you pay.

Can I use wallet bonus credit on domains?

No. Bonus credit is for hosting, VPS, email and apps. It is not valid on domain registrations, transfers or renewals.

Does bonus credit expire?

Bonus credit is valid for 90 days from the top-up that earned it. The money you added yourself never expires.
