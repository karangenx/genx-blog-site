<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How the daily deals work</h2>

The pre-sale countdown was set to <strong>14 November 2026, 00:00 IST</strong>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Wallet bonus credit</h2>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the INR sale page, topping up your Gen X wallet during the sale adds bonus credit. The money you add never expires. The <strong>bonus</strong> is valid for <strong>90 days</strong> from that top-up, is applied to your next eligible invoice automatically, and cannot be withdrawn, transferred or refunded. If the add-funds payment is reversed, the bonus is removed. The published tiers are in the table above.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Use bonus credit on hosting, VPS, email and apps. It is not valid on domains, and the sale FAQ says it <strong>may not apply to items already bought at a deal price</strong>. Offer terms allow only one promo code per order unless an offer says it can be combined, so check checkout if you hoped to stack a daily deal and wallet credit.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You do not need to be an existing customer. <a href="https://genxwhosting.com/register.php">Register</a>, then top up from the <a href="https://genxwhosting.com/clientarea.php">client area</a> using <a href="https://genxwhosting.com/knowledgebase/132/How-to-Add-Funds-in-Client-Area.html">How to Add Funds</a>. The tier buttons were still locked when we checked, so use the sale page once the vault opens, and switch to INR first.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Every Gen X account also gets one free <a href="https://genxwhosting.com/pages/black-friday.php#bf-spin">Spin &amp; Win</a> a day, with no purchase needed. Credit prizes land in the account straight away and are used on the next eligible invoice. The wheel shows ₹25, ₹50, ₹100 or ₹250 credit, and also no-prize outcomes.</p>
